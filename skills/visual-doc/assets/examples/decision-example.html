      1 · Why now

      One support ticket in four is a forgotten password

      Of 1,240 tickets a month, 310 ask for a new password. Each takes about five minutes, and most come in the evening when the shop is busiest.

      Password310
      Delivery270
      Returns190

      Conclusion

      Password tickets are the largest group we can remove without hiring. The other reasons need changes in the warehouse.

      2 · What others do

      Shops our size offer both, not links alone

      We looked at three comparable shops. Two keep the password and add a link; the one that dropped passwords brought them back after customer complaints.

      Appendix · Details

      How the numbers were worked out

      The ticket estimate

      310 password tickets a month × 5 minutes = about 26 hours. Shops A and B reported 45% and 55% fewer password tickets after adding a link; we used the midpoint, 50%.

      Words used on this page
      Email link
      A one-time link sent to the customer's email; opening it signs them in.
      Ticket
      One customer request to support, by email or chat.
